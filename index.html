ERC Consolidator Grant - Grant No. 101171357

CARAVEL aims to transform the study of the brain vessel tree by devising a novel methodological framework to extract, model and analyse brain vasculature from multimodal neurovascular images, enabling the creation of the first multiresolution atlas of neurovascular ageing.
