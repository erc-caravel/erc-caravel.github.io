CARAVEL at MICCAI 2025

28 April 2026

Members of the CARAVEL team travelled to Daejeon, Republic of Korea, to present our work at MICCAI 2025, the 28th International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention.

We presented VesselVerse, a dataset and collaborative framework for vessel annotation, and had the opportunity to discuss our approach with researchers from across the medical imaging community. The feedback and discussions during the poster sessions were extremely valuable and are already shaping the next steps of our research.

Thank you to everyone who stopped by our poster and to the organisers of MICCAI 2025 for a great conference.
